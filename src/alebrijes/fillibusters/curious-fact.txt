While that's being thought through, a small aside: the word "quiz" is one of those words nobody's fully sure where it came from. One old, probably apocryphal story claims a Dublin theatre owner invented it overnight on a bet, painting it on walls across the city until everyone was asking what it meant. It's a nice story. It's most likely not true. But it's stuck around for over two hundred years anyway, which is its own kind of interesting.

Words do that sometimes -- outlive the story of where they came from, and just keep being useful long after anyone remembers why.
