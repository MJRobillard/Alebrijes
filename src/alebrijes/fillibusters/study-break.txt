Spaced repetition -- coming back to the same idea a few times, with a bit of a gap in between -- tends to beat cramming the same fact over and over in one sitting. The gap is what does the work: your memory has to reach a little to retrieve the idea again, and that small effort is what makes it stick.

If you find yourself unsure of an answer, it often helps to say out loud what you do remember about the topic, even if it's not the whole picture. Half-formed knowledge is still knowledge, and talking it through can nudge the rest of it back into reach. There's no rush here -- take the time you need.
